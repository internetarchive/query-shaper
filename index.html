<query-shaper>

<query-shaper> is a web component you attach to a search input to turn natural-language text into something your search backend can actually use — powered entirely by Chrome's on-device AI, so nothing a visitor types ever leaves their browser. It offers typo fixes, finished-out phrases, and broader related terms out of the box, and, once you describe your backend's fields, fielded/boolean reformulations too.
