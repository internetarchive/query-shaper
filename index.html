<query-shaper>

<query-shaper> is a web component you attach to a search input to turn what someone types into precise, fielded/boolean search syntax your backend can actually run — field:value, AND/OR, ranges — once you describe your backend's fields. It also fixes typos, finishes cut-off phrases, and broadens with related terms out of the box, all powered entirely by Chrome's on-device AI, so nothing a visitor types ever leaves their browser.
